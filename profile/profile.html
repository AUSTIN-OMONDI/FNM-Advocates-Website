About the firm

Who we are

FN Muthoni Advocates is a Nairobi-based law firm built on a simple belief: good legal work starts with listening.

We act for individuals, families, entrepreneurs, companies and institutions across Kenya. Whether a client is buying their first home, bringing in international talent, growing a business, planning for the next generation or resolving a dispute, we take the time to understand the full picture before we advise.

Our practice goes beyond the traditional. Our advocates are Certified Professional Mediators and Data Protection Officers — so we can help clients settle disagreements without years in court, and help organisations meet their obligations under the Data Protection Act, 2019.

The result is counsel that is rigorous in substance, plain in language and always focused on the outcome that serves the client best.

Data Protection & Privacy

Every organisation that collects personal data — customers, patients, students, staff or members — now carries legal duties under the Data Protection Act, 2019.

As certified Data Protection Officers, we make compliance practical and proportionate. We help businesses, schools, clinics, SACCOs, NGOs and faith-based organisations register with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC), put the right policies in place and respond confidently if something goes wrong.

Our compliance services

  • Data protection impact assessments
  • Privacy policies, notices and consent forms
  • Data sharing and processing agreements
  • Staff training and awareness
  • Data breach response planning
  • Outsourced Data Protection Officer service

How we work

A clear, predictable process — so you always know where your matter stands and what happens next.

1

Consultation

We meet in person or online to understand your situation, your goals and your concerns. Everything you share is confidential.

2

Advice & fee guidance

You receive honest, plain-language advice on your options, the likely timeline and the expected cost before any work begins.

3

Engagement

Once you are ready, we confirm our instructions in writing and set out exactly what we will do for you.

4

Action

We draft, negotiate, mediate, file or litigate — whichever route best serves your interests — keeping you updated at each milestone.

5

Completion & aftercare

We close the matter properly, hand over all documents and remain available for whatever you need next.

Fair, transparent fees

Our fees are guided by the Advocates (Remuneration) Order. Where appropriate we offer fixed fees, so you can plan with certainty.

Easy to reach

Call, email or WhatsApp us. Consultations are available in person in Nairobi or virtually, wherever you are.
