Beyond the courtroom

Two specialisms that set us apart

Many disputes don't need years in court, and every organisation that handles personal data now has legal duties. We're certified in both.

Certified Professional Mediators

Mediation is confidential, quicker and usually far less costly than litigation — and it preserves relationships that matter, in business and in families.

  • Commercial and contractual disputes
  • Family, succession and estate disagreements
  • Land and boundary disputes
  • Workplace and employment conflicts
  • Representation in court-annexed mediation

Data Protection Officers

The Data Protection Act, 2019 applies to any business, school, clinic, SACCO or NGO that collects personal data. We help you comply — and stay compliant.

  • Registration with the ODPC as controller or processor
  • Data protection impact assessments
  • Privacy policies, notices and consent forms
  • Staff training and data breach response plans
  • Outsourced Data Protection Officer (DPO) service

Ask a Question
How do I book a consultation?

Call or WhatsApp us on 011 9592394, email fnmuthoni.law@gmail.com, or use the enquiry form below. We'll confirm a time that suits you, in person or online.

Can you commission an affidavit or notarise a document today?

Yes. As Commissioner for Oaths and Notary Public we handle affidavits, statutory declarations and notarisation of documents for local and international use. Bring original identification and the documents, unsigned.

What is mediation and is it binding?

Mediation is a confidential process where a neutral, certified mediator helps the parties reach their own agreement. Once the parties sign a settlement agreement it can be binding, and in court-annexed mediation it can be adopted as an order of the court.

Does the Data Protection Act apply to my small business?

If you collect personal data — customer names, phone numbers, staff records, patient or student files — the Act applies to you. Registration thresholds depend on your sector and size; we can assess your position quickly.

How are your fees charged?

Fees are guided by the Advocates (Remuneration) Order and depend on the nature of the matter. We explain the expected fees before any work starts, so there are no surprises.
